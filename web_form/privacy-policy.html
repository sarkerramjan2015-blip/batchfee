BatchFee Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

1. Introduction

BatchFee ("we," "our," or "us") is a SaaS education management platform designed for institutes, tutors, and academies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use the BatchFee mobile application ("the App").

By using BatchFee, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Who We Serve

BatchFee is built for educational institutes (coaching centers, private tutors, academies, schools) to manage their students, staff, fees, attendance, and exams. The App is used by:

  • Institute Owners & Administrators — who manage the institute's operations
  • Staff Members (teachers, accountants) — who perform specific roles within the institute

Separately, a public web registration form allows prospective students (or their guardians) to submit admission inquiries to institutes. That form, hosted at our Firebase Hosting domain, also falls under this policy.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Account Information

When an institute owner, admin, or staff member uses the App, we collect:

  • Email address (used for Firebase Authentication login)
  • Password (stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash on-device; authentication via Firebase Auth)
  • Role within the institute (Owner, Admin, Staff)

3.2 Institute Profile Data

When an institute registers, we collect and store in Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • Institute name, owner name, phone number, WhatsApp number, address
  • Subscription plan and payment status
  • Institute profile photo (optional)

3.3 Staff Data

Institute owners/admins can add staff profiles containing:

  • Full name, staff code, job title, phone number, email address, address
  • Monthly salary, joining date, assigned role and permissions
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Attendance records

3.4 Student Data (Local Only)

Institutes enter student information into the App which is stored locally on the device only and is NOT uploaded to cloud servers:

  • Full name, student code, photo, gender, date of birth
  • Phone number, email address, residential address
  • School name, class/grade
  • Guardian name, phone number, email
  • Emergency contact, blood group
  • Admission date, enrollment history
  • Fee payment history, attendance records, exam results

This data stays on the device. It is only synced to cloud storage if the institute owner explicitly uses the backup/restore feature.

3.5 Web Registration Form (Public)

Our public registration form, used by students/guardians, collects and stores in Firebase Firestore:

  • Student full name, phone number, guardian name, WhatsApp number
  • Gender, date of birth, school name, class, address
  • These submissions are stored in the institute's Firestore subcollection and are only accessible to the respective institute's authenticated owner/admin.

3.6 Demo & Trial Users

BatchFee offers an in-app demo mode. When a demo is launched:

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication is used (no email/password required)
  • Device model, platform (Android), and timestamp are stored in Firestore under Demo_Visitors
  • No personal identity is linked to demo sessions

3.7 Crash & Performance Data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, which may include:

  • Device model, Android OS version, app version
  • Stack traces at the time of a crash
  • This data helps us identify and fix bugs. No personal information is intentionally collected in crash reports.

3.8 Subscription & Payment Data

When an institute submits a subscription upgrade request:

  • Plan selection, duration, and payment method are stored
  • Last 4 digits of transaction reference may be stored for verification
  • We do NOT process or store full payment card details. All payments are handled externally by the institute's chosen payment method (bKash, Nagad, bank transfer).

4. Device Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • Camera — Optional. Used only when you explicitly choose to capture a student/staff profile photo or expense receipt attachment. Photos are stored on-device.
  • Biometric (Fingerprint/Face) — Used for app login convenience. Biometric data is processed entirely by the Android operating system; the App never receives or stores fingerprint/face data. Only an encrypted session token is stored in device SharedPreferences.

We do NOT request or access: Location, contacts, SMS, storage (beyond app-specific directory), microphone, or advertising ID.

5. Third-Party Services

BatchFee relies on the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Integrity — Used (when enabled) to verify the App is running on a genuine Android device

Data stored in Firebase Firestore resides on Google Cloud infrastructure. We do not share your data with any other third parties, sell data, or use data for advertising.

6. How We Use Data

All data collected is used solely to provide and improve the BatchFee service:

  • Authenticating users via Firebase Auth
  • Storing institute, staff, and registration data to enable institute management features
  • Syncing institute profile and staff data across devices logged into the same account (via Firestore)
  • Analyzing crash reports to fix bugs (Crashlytics)
  • Tracking demo usage to improve the product

7. Data Storage & Retention

  • On-device data (student records, fees, attendance, exams, expenses, salaries) — stored in a local Room (SQLite) database. It persists until the App is uninstalled or the user explicitly clears app data.
  • Cloud data (institute profile, staff records, web registrations, subscription requests) — stored in Firebase Firestore. Retained as long as the institute maintains an active account. Institute owners may request deletion by contacting us.
  • Crash data — retained by Firebase for 90 days by default.

8. Data Export & Deletion

The App includes a built-in Export All Data feature (in Settings) that generates a CSV file containing all student, batch, fee, payment, expense, staff, and enquiry records stored on-device. This file is shared via the Android share sheet at your discretion.

To request deletion of cloud-stored data (Firestore), contact us at the email listed below.

9. Children's Privacy

BatchFee is a tool used by educational institutes to manage their operations. The App itself is not directed at children. Any student data entered into the App is entered by adult institute staff members in the course of managing their educational institution. The public web registration form may be filled out by students or their guardians; the data collected is limited to admission-relevant information and is provided to the institute directly.

10. Security

We implement security measures including:

  • Firebase Authentication for secure login
  • Firebase Security Rules restricting Firestore access to authorized users only
  • One-way password hashing (SHA-256) for local password storage
  • Biometric authentication using Android's BiometricPrompt API (no raw biometric data stored)
  • Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) to verify genuine app instances (when enabled)

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International Data Transfers

Firebase services are operated by Google LLC and data may be processed on servers located in various countries. By using BatchFee, you consent to this transfer and processing in accordance with Google's data processing terms.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on our website. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
